# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Reactor users run cross-source research, complex analysis, and agent tasks from a conversation workspace. They need to follow execution, inspect live desktop activity, and review the resulting files and reports without losing the current task context.

## Product Purpose

Reactor is a cloud agent workbench for research and complex task execution. It combines public and private information sources, agent workflows, sandboxed tools, and deliverables such as reports and interactive outputs. Success means users can direct the work, understand its current state, and inspect its results in the same session.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, clear, and reliable. The interface should feel focused on completing and inspecting work rather than presenting the product.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style layouts, decorative card stacks, and tool controls detached from the task context.

## Design Principles

- Keep instructions and human decisions in the conversation; keep live state, previews, and inspectable outputs in the workspace.
- Make execution state and deliverables visible where users review the work.
- Preserve task context while users move between the conversation and workspace.
- Keep controls close to the conversation that requested them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, and respect reduced-motion preferences. No additional formal conformance target was specified.
